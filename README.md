# HFTC campus snow globe

A low-poly, PS1-style 3D model of Hayward Fire Station #6 & Fire Training
Center on a red plinth, like a snow globe without the glass, with snow or
smoke/ash weather. Built from the city's published construction drawings.
Static page: `index.html`, `main.js`, the model in `assets/`, and
vendored three.js r170 in `vendor/` (MIT, `vendor/THREE-LICENSE`).

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap or click to shake. The buttons
switch the PS1 effects, cycle the weather and download the `.glb`.

URL params: `?weather=snow|smoke|none`, `?focus=B1`..`B9`,
`?az=<deg>&el=<deg>&dist=<n>`, `?ps1=0`, `?spin=<n>`.

Live: https://nicksramcik.github.io/hftc-globe/
